Lesson Plan: Subtraction of Integers

Topic: The "Add the Opposite" (KCC: Keep-Change-Change) Principle, Transforming Subtraction to Addition, and Resolving Double Negatives.

1. Learning Objectives

  • Cognitive: Explain and apply the Keep-Change-Change (KCC) principle to correctly transform integer subtraction expressions into equivalent addition forms.
  • Affective: Appreciate the efficiency of mathematical rules in simplifying complex signed-number expressions.
  • Psychomotor: Construct step-by-step written transformations for subtraction of integers, correctly resolving double negatives.

2. The Core Principle: Keep-Change-Change (KCC)

Directly subtracting signed numbers can be confusing. Instead, we convert every subtraction problem into an addition problem using the Keep-Change-Change (KCC) rule, also known as "adding the opposite":

  • KEEP the first number (the minuend) exactly as it is.
  • CHANGE the subtraction sign (-) to an addition sign (+).
  • CHANGE the second number (the subtrahend) to its opposite sign (its additive inverse).

3. Applying KCC & Resolving Double Negatives

Example 1 (Standard Subtraction): 5 - 12 = ?

  1. Keep the first number: 5
  2. Change minus to plus: +
  3. Change 12 to its opposite: -12
  4. New Expression: 5 + (-12)
  5. Solve using addition rules: Different signs, so subtract absolute values (12 - 5 = 7) and keep the sign of the larger absolute value (-12).

Result: 5 - 12 = -7

Example 2 (Resolving Double Negatives): (-8) - (-3) = ?

  1. Keep the first number: -8
  2. Change minus to plus: +
  3. Change -3 to its opposite: +3 (Note how subtracting a negative becomes adding a positive).
  4. New Expression: (-8) + 3
  5. Solve: 8 - 3 = 5, keeping the negative sign of the larger absolute value.

Result: (-8) - (-3) = -5

4. Localized Real-World Application

Temperature Drop Difference: Suppose the afternoon high temperature in a highland region was +4°C, and by late evening it dropped significantly to -5°C. To find the total change or difference between the high and low points:

Expression: (+4) - (-5)

Applying KCC: 4 + (+5) = 9. The total temperature interval span is 9 degrees.

💡 Formative Self-Check Quiz

  1. Evaluate using KCC: 10 - (-6)
    Answer: 16 (Transforms to 10 + 6 = 16)
  2. Evaluate using KCC: (-14) - 5
    Answer: -19 (Transforms to (-14) + (-5) = -19)
