DepEd ARAL Math Intervention Program • Key Stage 3

ILAW Lesson Plan: Understanding Signed Numbers

Grade Level: Key Stage 3 (Grades 7–10)
Quarter/Unit: Term 1 • Unit 1.1
Duration: 60 minutes
Learning Area: ARAL Mathematics

Learning Objectives (DepEd Competencies)

  • Cognitive: Identify, compare, and order positive integers, negative integers, and zero on the real number line.
  • Affective: Express confidence in connecting negative numbers to real-world financial and environmental situations.
  • Psychomotor: Plot real-world values correctly on a vertical and horizontal number line.
I
INTRODUCE (Hook, Drill & Objectives) • 10 Mins

1. Drill / Review Activity

  • Quick warm-up on natural numbers and zero using simple counting cards.
  • Ask students: "What is the lowest possible number you can think of?"

2. Motivational Hook (Real-World Context)

  • Present two opposite scenarios on the board:
    • Scenario A: Earning ₱500 profit in a sari-sari store.
    • Scenario B: Having a debt of ₱500 at the store.
  • Ask: "How can we represent these two opposite financial situations using mathematics?"

3. Unpacking Objectives

State clearly that today's goal is to understand numbers below zero (signed numbers) and plot them on a number line.

L
LEARN (Direct Instruction & Concept Development) • 20 Mins

1. Core Concept Exploration

  • Signed Numbers: Explain that signed numbers consist of positive numbers (+), negative numbers (-), and zero (which is neutral).
  • The Real Number Line: Draw a horizontal number line.
    • Zero is the origin/anchor point.
    • Positive integers sit to the RIGHT of zero.
    • Negative integers sit to the LEFT of zero.
Teacher Key Demonstration:
Show vertical contexts: Above sea level (+100m) vs. Below sea level (-50m).
Emphasize: As you move to the right (or up), value increases. As you move to the left (or down), value decreases (e.g., -5 is smaller than -2).

2. Guided Examples

  1. Compare -8 and 3: Since 3 is to the right of -8 on the number line, 3 > -8.
  2. Compare -10 and -2: Since -2 is closer to 0 (further right), -2 > -10.
A
APPLY (Guided & Independent Practice) • 20 Mins

1. Pair-Share Activity (Guided Practice)

Distribute mini-number line boards. Have pairs plot and translate these real-world phrases into signed integers:

  • a 12-degree drop in temperature (-12)
  • a deposit of ₱1,500 (+1500)
  • 50 meters below sea level (-50)

2. Independent Activity (Individual Work)

Task students to arrange the following sets of integers in ascending order (least to greatest):

  • Set A: {-4, 0, 7, -9, 2} → Answer: {-9, -4, 0, 2, 7}
  • Set B: {-15, -3, -20, 1, -1} → Answer: {-20, -15, -3, -1, 1}
W
WRAP-UP (Generalization & Formative Assessment) • 10 Mins

1. Student Generalization

Ask 2-3 learners to complete these sentences:

  • "Numbers to the left of zero are always..."
  • "Between two negative numbers, the one closer to zero is..."

2. Formative Assessment (Exit Ticket)

Write the correct symbol (<, >, or =) on a 1/4 sheet of paper:

  1. -7 ____ -2
  2. 0 ____ -5
  3. +12 ____ -12
