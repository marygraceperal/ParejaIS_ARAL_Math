Lesson Plan: The Distributive Property

Topic 2.3: Expanding a(b + c) and a(b - c) Across Signed Integers & Area Model Visualization (ILAW Framework)

I – ILLUMINATE & INTRODUCE

1. Learning Objectives

  • Cognitive: Apply the Distributive Property to expand expressions of the form a(b + c) and a(b - c) involving positive and negative integers.
  • Affective: Appreciate how geometric models bridge abstract algebraic rules to concrete physical representations.
  • Psychomotor: Sketch and label grid area models to visually demonstrate expanded multiplication terms.

2. Initiating Hook

Ask students: "If you buy 6 packs of supplies containing 10 notebooks and 2 pens each, how do you find the total items? Do you calculate 6 × 12, or do you calculate (6 × 10) + (6 × 2)? Notice how both give 72 items!" Introduce this as the core principle of distribution.

L – LEARN

1. Core Algebraic Definitions

  • Distributive Property of Multiplication over Addition:
    a(b + c) = ab + ac
  • Distributive Property of Multiplication over Subtraction:
    a(b - c) = ab - ac

2. Area Model Representation

Multiplication can be visualized as finding the area of a rectangle. When a length is split into two parts, the total area equals the sum of the sub-areas.

Visualizing 4(10 + 3) = 4(10) + 4(3)

10 +3
4 Area = 40 Area = 12

Total Area = 40 + 12 = 52

⚠️ Crucial Pitfall Warning: Distributing Negative Signs

When the factor outside the parentheses is negative, it must be distributed to EVERY term inside, remembering that a negative times a negative produces a positive:
-3(x - 5) = -3(x) - (-3)(5) = -3x + 15 (NOT -3x - 15!)

3. Guided Step-by-Step Examples

Example 1: Expanding with Positive Factors

Expand: 5(2x - 7)

  1. Distribute 5 to the first term: 5 × 2x = 10x
  2. Distribute 5 to the second term: 5 × (-7) = -35
  3. Combine terms: 10x - 35

Result: 10x - 35

Example 2: Expanding with Negative Factors

Expand: -4(-3a + 6)

  1. Distribute -4 to first term: (-4) × (-3a) = +12a
  2. Distribute -4 to second term: (-4) × (+6) = -24
  3. Combine terms: 12a - 24

Result: 12a - 24

A – APPLY

Localized Real-World Applications

Application 1: Fast Mental Calculation (Retail Pricing)

A bakery sells 7 specialty cake boxes at ₱198 each.

Mental Shortcut using Distribution: Think of ₱198 as (200 - 2).

7 × (200 - 2) = (7 × 200) - (7 × 2) = 1,400 - 14 = ₱1,386

Application 2: Multi-Item Budgeting

An office buys 5 employee care packages. Each package contains an item costing ₱x minus a promotional discount of ₱15.

Total Cost Expression: 5(x - 15) = 5x - 75 pesos.

W – WIND-UP

1. Formative Self-Check Quiz

  1. Expand: -6(2m - 5)
    Answer: -12m + 30
  2. Calculate Mentally: 8 × 99 using distribution.
    Answer: 8(100 - 1) = 800 - 8 = 792
  3. Area Model Check: A rectangle has a height of 3 and a length split into x and 4. What is its expanded area?
    Answer: 3x + 12

2. Key Takeaways Summary

  • Distribution Means Sharing: Multiply the outer term by every term inside the parentheses.
  • Sign Rules Apply: Pay special attention when distributing negative numbers ((-) × (-) = (+)).
  • Area Models: Provide a visual layout to verify that a(b + c) equals the sum of individual sub-areas (ab + ac).
