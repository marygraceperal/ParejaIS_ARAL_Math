Teacher Answer Key

Part I: Keyword Classification

  • Addition (+): sum, increased by
  • Subtraction (Direct Order): decreased by, subtracted by
  • Subtraction ("Turn-Around"): subtracted from, less than
  • Multiplication (×): twice, multiplied by, product of
  • Division (÷): ratio of, quotient of, per

Part II: Direct Translation vs. "Turn-Around"

  1. x + 12
  2. 15 − y
  3. y − 15
  4. 6n
  5. w − 8
  6. 8 − w
  7. p / 4
  8. 3m − 5

Part III: Spot the Error!

  1. Incorrect. Correction: x − 7.
    Reason: "Less than" is a turn-around phrase, so 7 is subtracted from x.
  2. Incorrect. Correction: 12 / b.
    Reason: In "quotient of 12 and b", the first variable listed (12) is the dividend (numerator).
  3. Incorrect. Correction: 4(x + 9).
    Reason: The word "sum" acts as a container, grouping (x + 9) inside parentheses before multiplying by 4.
