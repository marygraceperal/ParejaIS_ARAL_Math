Part III: Context Creation Sample Answers

  • 1. Sample Answer (A = 12x + 50): A bakery charges ₱12 per box of bread x plus a flat delivery fee of ₱50. The total amount paid is A.
  • 2. Sample Answer (T = 1000 − 120n): Maria has a ₱1,000 allowance and spends ₱120 per day for n days. T represents her remaining balance.
