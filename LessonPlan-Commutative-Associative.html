Lesson Plan: Commutative & Associative Properties

Topic 2.1: Rearranging and Regrouping Addition and Multiplication Terms to Simplify Mental Math Calculations (ILAW Framework)

I – ILLUMINATE & INTRODUCE

1. Learning Objectives

  • Cognitive: Identify and apply the Commutative and Associative Properties of addition and multiplication to simplify multi-term numerical calculations mentally.
  • Affective: Value efficiency and flexible thinking when strategizing mathematical solutions in daily financial and operational tasks.
  • Psychomotor: Reorder and regroup multi-term expressions on work boards to clearly demonstrate step-by-step mental shortcuts.

2. Initiating Hook

Ask students: "If you need to add ₱48 + ₱37 + ₱52 + ₱63 in your head while standing at a checkout counter, would you add them in exact order from left to right, or would you pair ₱48 with ₱52 first? Why?" Highlight how changing order and grouping lets us work with friendly multiples of 10 or 100.

L – LEARN

1. Core Properties Definitions

  • Commutative Property (Order Change): Changing the order of the numbers being added or multiplied does not change the result.
    a + b = b + a  |  a × b = b × a
  • Associative Property (Grouping Change): Changing the grouping (parentheses) of numbers being added or multiplied does not change the result.
    (a + b) + c = a + (b + c)  |  (a × b) × c = a × (b × c)

⚠️ Crucial Pitfall Warning: Subtraction and Division are NOT Commutative or Associative!

• 10 - 4 ≠ 4 - 10 (Order matters!)
• (12 ÷ 4) ÷ 2 ≠ 12 ÷ (4 ÷ 2) (Grouping matters!)

2. Step-by-Step Mental Math Strategies

Example 1 (Addition — Compatible Pairs to 100):

Evaluate mentally: 48 + 37 + 52 + 63

  1. Rearrange (Commutative): Move compatible terms next to each other: 48 + 52 + 37 + 63
  2. Regroup (Associative): Group terms that sum to clean hundreds: (48 + 52) + (37 + 63)
  3. Compute: 100 + 100 = 200

Result: 200

Example 2 (Multiplication — Power of 10 Benchmark):

Evaluate mentally: 4 × 19 × 25

  1. Rearrange (Commutative): Swap terms to pair factors that multiply to 100: 4 × 25 × 19
  2. Regroup (Associative): Group the benchmark product: (4 × 25) × 19
  3. Compute: 100 × 19 = 1,900

Result: 1,900

A – APPLY

Localized Real-World Application

Retail Store Batch Supply Tally: A local store owner purchases inventory supplies consisting of 4 item batches priced at ₱125, ₱84, ₱75, and ₱116.

Standard Sequential Addition: 125 + 84 = 209; 209 + 75 = 284; 284 + 116 = 400 (Prone to mental fatigue).

Strategic Rearranging & Regrouping:

  1. Pair 125 + 75 = 200
  2. Pair 84 + 116 = 200
  3. Combine: 200 + 200 = ₱400

Application: Using Commutative and Associative properties transforms complex multi-digit additions into instantaneous, error-free calculations.

W – WIND-UP

1. Formative Self-Check Quiz

  1. Simplify Mentally: 25 × 7 × 4
    Answer: 700 (Rearrange to (25 × 4) × 7 = 100 × 7).
  2. Identify Property: Which property justifies writing (14 + 29) + 86 as 29 + (14 + 86)?
    Answer: Both Commutative (swapping 14 and 29) and Associative (regrouping 14 and 86).

2. Synthesis & Key Takeaways

  • Commutative = Swap Order: Move friendly numbers next to each other.
  • Associative = Change Groups: Group terms into multiples of 10, 100, or 1,000 before proceeding.
  • Scope: Use freely for addition and multiplication; never apply to subtraction or division.
