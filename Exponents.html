Topic 3.2: Exponent Basics & Signed Bases

Discussion Guide: Expanded Form, Zero Exponents, and Negation Rules

1. Exponent Fundamentals & Expanded Notation

An exponential expression consists of two key components: a base (b) and an exponent (n), written in the form bn.

Definition: For any positive integer n, bn represents repeated multiplication of the base b by itself n times.

bn = b × b × b × ... × b (n factors)

Examples of Expanded Form:

  • 53 = 5 × 5 × 5 = 125
  • 25 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32
  • (x)4 = x × x × x × x
2. The Zero Exponent Rule (x⁰ = 1)

A common point of confusion for students is why any non-zero number raised to the power of zero equals 1.

Zero Exponent Rule: For any real number x ≠ 0,

x0 = 1

Note: 00 is an indeterminate form (undefined in elementary algebra).

Conceptual Proof via Pattern Analysis:

Consider powers of 2 decreasing sequentially:

Expression Value Pattern / Operation
24 16 Initial value
23 8 Divide by 2
22 4 Divide by 2
21 2 Divide by 2
20 1 2 ÷ 2 = 1
3. Signed Bases: Odd vs. Even Powers

When the base of an exponential term is negative and enclosed in parentheses, the parity (even or odd) of the exponent dictates the sign of the final product.

Exponent Parity Rule Formula Expanded Example Final Sign
Even Power (-a)even = +an (-3)4 = (-3)(-3)(-3)(-3) Positive (+81)
Odd Power (-a)odd = -an (-3)3 = (-3)(-3)(-3) Negative (-27)

Why? Negative signs pair up in multiples of two to produce positive values (- × - = +). An odd exponent leaves one unpaired negative factor.

4. Critical Diagnostic Pitfall: Parentheses Matter!

⚠️ Parentheses vs. No Parentheses

Students frequently confuse (-x)n with -xn. The order of operations (PEMDAS) treats them completely differently:

  • (-2)4: The base is -2. Multiply -2 four times:
    (-2) × (-2) × (-2) × (-2) = +16
  • -24: The base is 2, NOT -2. Exponents apply BEFORE negation!
    -(2 × 2 × 2 × 2) = -16
Expression Base Interpretation Result
(-5)2 -5 Negative 5 squared 25
-52 5 Opposite of (5 squared) -25
(-1)100 -1 Even power of negative base 1
-1100 1 Opposite of (1 to power 100) -1
5. Formative Check & Discussion Wrap-Up

Quick Practice Questions:

  1. Evaluate (-4)3
    Answer: (-4)(-4)(-4) = -64
  2. Evaluate -30 + (-3)0
    Answer: -(1) + (1) = -1 + 1 = 0
  3. Evaluate (-2)4 - 24
    Answer: 16 - 16 = 0
