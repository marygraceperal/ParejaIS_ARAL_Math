Lesson: Multiplication & Division of Signed Numbers

Understand the golden rules for multiplying and dividing integers, including repeated operations with multiple negative factors.

1. The Two Basic Rules for Signed Numbers

When multiplying or dividing two signed numbers, the sign of the result depends entirely on whether the signs match or differ.

Signs involved Operation Result Sign Example
Same Signs ( Positive & Positive ) Multiplication / Division POSITIVE (+) 6 × 3 = 18
Same Signs ( Negative & Negative ) Multiplication / Division POSITIVE (+) (-6) × (-3) = +18
Different Signs ( Positive & Negative ) Multiplication / Division NEGATIVE (−) 8 × (-2) = -16
Different Signs ( Negative & Positive ) Multiplication / Division NEGATIVE (−) (-20) ÷ 4 = -5
Summary Rule:
  • Like signs give a positive result: (+) × (+) = + and (−) × (−) = +
  • Unlike signs give a negative result: (+) × (−) = − and (−) × (+) = −

2. Repeated Multiplication & Counting Negatives

When multiplying three or more numbers together, you can determine the sign of the final answer quickly by counting the total number of negative factors:

  • If there is an EVEN count of negative numbers, the product is POSITIVE (+).
  • If there is an ODD count of negative numbers, the product is NEGATIVE (−).

Example A (Even count): (−2) × (−3) × (−1) × (−4)

  • Count the negatives: 4 negative factors (4 is even).
  • Multiply values: 2 × 3 × 1 × 4 = 24
  • Final Answer: +24

Example B (Odd count): (−2) × 3 × (−5) × (−1)

  • Count the negatives: 3 negative factors (3 is odd).
  • Multiply values: 2 × 3 × 5 × 1 = 30
  • Final Answer: −30

3. Division Involving Zero

Keep these fundamental properties in mind when dividing with zero:

  • 0 ÷ a = 0 (Zero divided by any non-zero number is always 0).
  • a ÷ 0 = Undefined (Division by zero is undefined in mathematics).

💡 Quick Self-Check Quiz

  1. Solve: (−8) × (−7)
    Answer: +56 (Same signs produce a positive result)
  2. Solve: 45 ÷ (−9)
    Answer: −5 (Different signs produce a negative result)
  3. Solve: (−2) × (−3) × (−4)
    Answer: −24 (Odd count of 3 negative factors)
