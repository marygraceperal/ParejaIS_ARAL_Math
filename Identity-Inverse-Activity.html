Student Activity: Identity & Inverse Properties

Topic 2.2: Additive/Multiplicative Identities (0 & 1) and Inverses (Zero Pairs & Reciprocals)

📌 Instructions

Solve each section carefully. Identify whether the given equation illustrates an Identity or Inverse property, calculate zero pairs and reciprocals, and simplify algebraic expressions step-by-step.

Part I: Property Identification

1. Identify the exact property shown in each statement:

a) -18 + 18 = 0

b) (5/9) × 1 = 5/9

c) 14 × (1/14) = 1

d) -45 + 0 = -45

Part II: Finding Additive & Multiplicative Inverses

2. For each number below, write its Additive Inverse (Opposite) and its Multiplicative Inverse (Reciprocal):

a) 9

b) -4/7

c) 1

Part III: Simplifying Expressions with Properties

3. Use Identity and Inverse properties to evaluate the expressions mentally. Show your simplification steps:

a) 37 + (-37) + 0 + 19

b) (4/11) × 23 × (11/4) × 1

Part IV: Financial Balance Application

4. A school store ledger records the following transactions for the day: a cash credit of ₱320, an immediate refund expense of -₱320, a sales addition of ₱0, and an inventory payout of ₱150.

Write an equation representing this scenario, circle the zero pair, and determine the final balance using identity laws.
