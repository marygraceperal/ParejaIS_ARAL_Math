Lesson Plan: Addition of Integers

Topic 1.2: Visualizing with Algebra Tiles and Number Lines, Combining Same-Sign Integers, and Applying Rules for Different Signs (ILAW Framework)

I – ILLUMINATE & INTRODUCE

1. Learning Objectives

  • Cognitive: Illustrate and calculate the sum of signed integers using algebra tiles, number lines, and mathematical rules.
  • Affective: Appreciate the precision and logic of signed numbers in modeling real-world economic and environmental quantities.
  • Psychomotor: Construct visual models (number lines and tile groupings) to correctly demonstrate integer addition steps.

2. Initiating Hook

Introduce the concept of financial gain (deposits/sales) and loss (debts/credits). Ask students: "If you owe a store owner money and then earn cash, how do you determine your actual remaining balance?"

L – LEARN

1. Visualizing Integer Addition

A. Algebra Tiles & Zero Pairs: Positive counters (+) represent positive values, while negative counters (−) represent negative values. When a positive counter is paired with a negative counter, they form a zero pair (canceling each other out to equal zero).

B. Number Lines: Start at the first addend on the number line. Move to the right if the second addend is positive, or move to the left if the second addend is negative.

2. Core Rule 1: Adding Same-Sign Integers

Rule: Add their absolute values (disregarding the signs), and keep the common sign for the result.

Example 1 (Negative + Negative): (-7) + (-4) = ?

  1. Find absolute values: |-7| = 7 and |-4| = 4.
  2. Add values: 7 + 4 = 11.
  3. Keep the shared negative sign: -11.

Result: (-7) + (-4) = -11

3. Core Rule 2: Adding Different-Sign Integers

Rule: Subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger absolute value, and copy the sign of the number with the larger absolute value.

Example 2 (Different Signs): (-12) + 5 = ?

  1. Find absolute values: |-12| = 12 and |5| = 5.
  2. Subtract smaller from larger: 12 - 5 = 7.
  3. Identify the sign of the larger absolute value: -12 has a larger absolute value than 5, so keep the negative sign.

Result: (-12) + 5 = -7

A – APPLY

Localized Real-World Application

Sari-Sari Store Cash Flow: A local micro-enterprise owner starts the morning with a deficit or credit balance. Suppose a ledger notes an initial credit adjustment of -₱250 due to a supplier advance, followed by a profitable cash injection of +₱400.

Expression: (-250) + 400

Application: Since the signs differ, subtract 400 - 250 = 150 and copy the sign of the larger absolute value (+400 is larger, so it is positive). The resulting net balance is +₱150.

W – WIND-UP

1. Formative Self-Check Quiz

  1. Evaluate: (-15) + (-8)
    Answer: -23 (Same sign rule: add 15 + 8 and keep the negative sign).
  2. Evaluate: 22 + (-9)
    Answer: +13 (Different sign rule: 22 - 9 = 13, copy positive sign of 22).

2. Synthesis & Key Takeaways

  • Same signs: Add absolute values and keep the sign.
  • Different signs: Subtract absolute values and take the sign of the larger absolute value.
