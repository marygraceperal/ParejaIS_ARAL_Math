Lesson: Subtraction of Integers

Master integer subtraction by converting it into addition using the Keep-Change-Change (KCC) rule and resolving double negatives with confidence.

1. The Core Principle: "Add the Opposite"

In mathematics, subtracting a number is exactly the same as adding its additive opposite. Instead of learning brand new rules for subtraction, we simply transform every subtraction problem into an addition problem!

The Golden Rule: a − b = a + (−b)
To subtract an integer, change the subtraction sign to addition and change the sign of the second integer.

2. The KCC Method (Keep - Change - Change)

To easily remember how to transform subtraction problems, use the KCC strategy:

1. KEEP

Keep the first integer exactly as it is.

2. CHANGE

Change the subtraction sign (−) to addition (+).

3. CHANGE

Change the sign of the second number to its opposite.

3. Resolving Double Negatives

What happens when you subtract a negative number? For example: 5 − (−3)

When two negative signs appear right next to each other separated only by parentheses, they combine to become a positive.

Example: Solve 5 − (−3)

  • KEEP the first number: 5
  • CHANGE subtraction to addition: +
  • CHANGE (−3) to its opposite: +3

Transformed Expression: 5 + 3 = 8

4. Step-by-Step Worked Examples

Example A: Subtracting a Positive Number

Problem: (−7) − 4

  1. Apply KCC: Keep (−7), Change (−) to (+), Change 4 to (−4).
  2. New problem: (−7) + (−4)
  3. Apply Addition Rules (Same Signs): Add absolute values (7 + 4 = 11) and keep the negative sign.
  4. Final Answer: −11

Example B: Subtracting a Larger Positive Number

Problem: 3 − 9

  1. Apply KCC: Keep 3, Change (−) to (+), Change 9 to (−9).
  2. New problem: 3 + (−9)
  3. Apply Addition Rules (Different Signs): Subtract absolute values (9 − 3 = 6) and copy the sign of the larger number (−9).
  4. Final Answer: −6

5. Real-World Applications

  • Temperature Differences: Finding the change in temperature from 5°C to −4°C requires subtraction: 5 − (−4) = 5 + 4 = 9°C drop.
  • Financial Debt Reduction: If a debt of ₱200 is removed (− −200) from your account, your financial standing increases by ₱200.
