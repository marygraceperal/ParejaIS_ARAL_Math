Topic 3.4: Multi-Operation Problem Solving

Discussion Guide: Evaluating Complex Expressions with Signed Bases, Fractions, and Absolute Value

1. Order of Operations & Critical Component Rules

Evaluating multi-operation numerical expressions requires strict adherence to the Order of Operations (PEMDAS/GEMDAS) while correctly applying sign and grouping rules:

  • Grouping Symbols (G): Treat absolute value bars |...| and main fraction bars as grouping symbols. Evaluate everything inside or on the top/bottom before proceeding outside.
  • Absolute Value Rules: |x| = x and |-x| = x. Absolute value measures distance from zero (always non-negative). Note that a negative sign outside absolute value bars remains negative: -| -5 | = -5.
  • Signed Bases with Exponents:
    • (-a)n: The base is negative. An even power yields a positive result, e.g., (-3)2 = 9.
    • -an: The base is positive; the negative sign is applied after exponentiation, e.g., -32 = -9.
  • Complex Fraction Bars: Evaluate the entire numerator and denominator separately before performing the final division.
2. Model Demonstration & Detailed Solution Path

Consider the complex numerical expression:

\frac{| -4^2 + 7 | - (-3)^3}{\frac{1}{2} - \left(-\frac{3}{4}\right)}

Step-by-Step Evaluation Walkthrough

Step Expression State Operation / Justification
1 Numerator: | -16 + 7 | - (-27) Evaluate exponent -42 = -16 inside absolute value and evaluate signed power (-3)3 = -27.
2 Numerator: | -9 | + 27 Simplify inside absolute value: -16 + 7 = -9. Change subtracting a negative to adding positive: - (-27) = + 27.
3 Numerator: 9 + 27 = 36 Apply absolute value: |-9| = 9. Add to complete numerator.
4 Denominator: \frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{2}{4} + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{5}{4} Change double negative to positive, find common denominator (4), and add fractions.
5 Final Division: 36 ÷ \frac{5}{4} = 36 · \frac{4}{5} Multiply numerator by the reciprocal of the denominator.
6 Final Result: \frac{144}{5} = 28.8 Perform multiplication: 36 · 4 = 144.
3. Diagnostic Misconceptions & Classroom Pitfalls

⚠️ Pitfall 1: Distributing Over Absolute Value Bars

Incorrect: -2 | 3 - 8 | → |-6 + 16| = 10

Correct: Absolute value acts as a strict grouping barrier. Evaluate inside first: -2 | -5 | = -2(5) = -10.

⚠️ Pitfall 2: Negating the Base vs. Negating the Power

Incorrect: Treating -52 as (-5) · (-5) = 25.

Correct: -52 = -(5 · 5) = -25. Parentheses explicitly dictate if the negative sign belongs to the base.

4. Formative Check & Discussion Wrap-Up

Quick Discussion Prompts:

  1. Evaluate: |-12| - (-2)^2
    Answer: 12 - 4 = 8
  2. Evaluate: \frac{-3^2 + 5}{|-2|}
    Answer: \frac{-9 + 5}{2} = \frac{-4}{2} = -2
  3. Why is the main fraction bar considered a grouping symbol in order of operations?
    Answer: It separates the problem into an independent numerator expression and denominator expression that must both be simplified completely before carrying out division.
