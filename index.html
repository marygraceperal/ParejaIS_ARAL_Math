ARAL Math Program - Key Stage 3 Syllabus

DepEd Academic Recovery and Accessible Learning (RA 12028) • 3-Term Framework

Author: Mary Grace M. Peral • Integrated AI-Assisted Curriculum Design

Term 1: Number Systems & Algebraic Foundations

Focus: Mastering signed numbers, order of operations, and the transition from arithmetic to algebra.

Unit 1: Integers & Rules of Signs

1.1
Understanding Signed Numbers
The Real Number Line, direct Philippine economic applications (profit ₱ / loss ₱, elevation above/below sea level), and comparing/ordering integers.
📘 Plan 📊 Slides 📝 Activity 🖨️ PDF
1.2
Addition of Integers
Visualizing with algebra tiles/number lines, combining same-sign integers, and applying the "subtract and copy sign of larger absolute value" rule to financial transactions (₱).
📘 Plan 📊 Slides 📝 Activity 🖨️ PDF
1.3
Subtraction of Integers
The "Add the Opposite" (KCC: Keep-Change-Change) principle, transforming subtraction to addition, and resolving double negatives.
📘 Plan 📊 Slides 📝 Activity 🖨️ PDF
1.4
Multiplication & Division of Signed Numbers
Rules for same signs (positive product/quotient) and different signs (negative product/quotient), including repeated multiplication.
📘 Plan 📊 Slides 📝 Activity 🖨️ PDF
1.5
Real-World Signed Number Problems
Multi-step word problems involving temperature shifts, bank balances (₱), and elevation changes.
📘 Plan 📊 Slides 📝 Activity 🖨️ PDF

Commutative & Associative Properties

Identity & Inverse Properties
Additive/multiplicative identities (0 and 1) and additive/multiplicative inverses (zero pairs and reciprocals).

The Distributive Property
Expanding a(b + c) and a(b - c) across positive and negative integers, recognizing distribution as area models.

Property Identification

Absolute Value Concepts

Order of Operations (GEMDAS)
Simplifying expressions with nested Grouping symbols, Exponents, Multiplication/Division, and Addition/Subtraction.

Multi-Operation Problem Solving
