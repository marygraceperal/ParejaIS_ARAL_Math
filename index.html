ARAL Math Program - Key Stage 3 Syllabus

DepEd Academic Recovery and Accessible Learning (RA 12028) • 3-Term Subtopic Framework

📌 Curriculum Overview & Context

All real-world scenarios, monetary applications, and problem sets are localized using the Philippine Peso (₱) context to ensure maximum clarity and relevance for learners.

Term 1: Number Systems & Algebraic Foundations

Focus: Mastering signed numbers, order of operations, and the transition from arithmetic to algebra.
