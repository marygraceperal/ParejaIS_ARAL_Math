Grade 11 Mathematics Lesson Plan

Topic 3.4: Multi-Operation Problem Solving (Signed Bases, Fractions & Absolute Value)

Grade Level: Grade 11
Curriculum: K-12 SHS Curriculum
Framework: ILAW Framework
Time Allotment: 60 Minutes

I. Learning Objectives

  • Cognitive: Evaluate complex numerical expressions incorporating signed bases, fractions, and absolute values using proper order of operations (GEMDAS).
  • Affective: Value accuracy, precision, and systematic step-by-step auditing when handling multi-step numerical calculations.
  • Psychomotor: Construct a structured multi-step evaluation path showing explicit simplification steps and corresponding mathematical rules.

II. Instructional Procedure (ILAW Framework)

I — INTRODUCE 10 Minutes

Hook & Practical Context

Present a real-world accounting audit scenario: Correcting an account ledger that contains debits (negative values), net balance checks (absolute value constraints), and fractional share distribution.

Guiding Question

"Why does calculating -42 versus (-4)2 yield drastically different financial balances in auditing software?"

Lesson Orientation

Establish that mathematical order of operations (GEMDAS) acts as an unbreakable protocol. A single misplaced sign or early evaluation leads to compounding calculation errors.

L — LEARN 20 Minutes

Direct Instruction & Operational Rules

  • Grouping Symbols (G): Treat absolute value bars |...| and main fraction bars as primary grouping barriers. Evaluate inside, above, or below first.
  • Signed Bases: Differentiate (-a)n (base is negative) from -an (negation occurs after exponentiation).
  • Absolute Value: |x| = x and |-x| = x. Remind students that -|-5| = -5 because the outer negative remains.

Modeling a Multi-Step Evaluation Path

Demonstrate solving on the board: E = [ |-42 + 7| - (-3)3 ] / [ 1/2 - (-3/4) ]

Step Expression State Operation / Rule Applied
1 Numerator: | -16 + 7 | - (-27) Evaluate exponent -42 = -16 & signed power (-3)3 = -27
2 Numerator: | -9 | + 27 Simplify inner absolute value -16 + 7 = -9; double negative to positive
3 Numerator: 9 + 27 = 36 Apply absolute value |-9| = 9 and sum numerator
4 Denominator: 1/2 + 3/4 = 2/4 + 3/4 = 5/4 Find common denominator and add fractions
5 Final Division: 36 × (4/5) = 144/5 = 28.8 Multiply numerator by denominator reciprocal
A — APPLY 20 Minutes

Collaborative Challenge: "Expression Decoders"

Divide the class into small groups. Distribute multi-operation expression cards containing signed bases, fraction bars, and absolute values.

Group Task

  • Construct a complete 5-step evaluation table for [ -32 + |-14 + 2| ] / [ (-2)3 + 5 ].
  • Identify and highlight potential trap points (e.g., distinguishing -32 from (-3)2).
  • Present their solution board to the class for peer verification.
W — WRAP-UP 10 Minutes

Synthesis & Misconception Clarification

Key Traps to Avoid:
  • Never distribute coefficients into absolute value bars (e.g., -2|-5| ≠ |10|). Evaluate inside first.
  • Parentheses dictate whether the negative sign is part of the base being raised to a power.

Formative Assessment (Exit Ticket)

  1. Evaluate: |-12| - (-2)2
  2. Evaluate: (-52 + 9) / |-2|
  3. Explain why -32 ≠ (-3)2 in 1–2 sentences.
