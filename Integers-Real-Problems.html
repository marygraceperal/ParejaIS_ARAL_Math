Lesson: Real-World Signed Number Problems

Learn to translate multi-step real-life scenarios into signed number mathematical expressions and solve them step by step.

1. Translating Keywords to Integer Signs

Real-world word problems often use specific context words to indicate positive and negative operations:

  • Positive (+) Keywords: Deposit, gain, rise, above sea level, elevation, profit, credit.
  • Negative (−) Keywords: Withdrawal, loss, drop, below sea level, depth, debt, fee, penalty.

2. Scenario 1: Bank Account Balances

Financial transactions are classic examples of multi-step signed number calculations.

Problem: Maria starts with a bank balance of ₱1,200. Over the week, she makes a withdrawal of ₱450, a deposit of ₱800, and incurs two equal service fees of ₱50 each. What is her final balance?

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Identify each term: Initial balance = +1200, Withdrawal = −450, Deposit = +800, Two fees = 2 × (−50) = −100
  2. Set up the expression: 1200 + (−450) + 800 + (−100)
  3. Combine positives & negatives:
    Positives: 1200 + 800 = 2000
    Negatives: (−450) + (−100) = −550
  4. Calculate final balance: 2000 + (−550) = 1450

Final Answer: Maria's final bank balance is ₱1,450.

3. Scenario 2: Multi-Step Temperature Shifts

Tracking temperature changes over time involves adding signed values and finding differences.

Problem: At midnight, the temperature in a high-altitude town was −3°C. By 6:00 AM, it dropped by 5°C. Over the next 6 hours, it rose by 12°C. Finally, a cold front caused it to drop by 8°C by evening. What was the final evening temperature?

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Identify each change: Start = −3, Drop = −5, Rise = +12, Drop = −8
  2. Set up the expression: (−3) + (−5) + 12 + (−8)
  3. Evaluate left to right:
    (−3) + (−5) = −8
    (−8) + 12 = +4
    4 + (−8) = −4

Final Answer: The final evening temperature was −4°C.

4. Scenario 3: Elevation & Depth Changes

Calculating overall elevation change when moving above and below sea level.

Problem: A researcher on a mountain is at an elevation of +150 meters. She hikes down 220 meters into a valley floor, then descends another 30 meters into an underground cavern. What is her final elevation relative to sea level?

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Identify each position/change: Start = +150, Descent 1 = −220, Descent 2 = −30
  2. Set up the expression: 150 + (−220) + (−30)
  3. Calculate:
    150 + (−220) = −70
    (−70) + (−30) = −100

Final Answer: Her final elevation is −100 meters (100 meters below sea level).

💡 Quick Self-Check Quiz

  1. Solve: An elevator starts on the 10th floor (+10), goes down 6 floors, goes down another 8 floors, and then moves up 3 floors. What floor is it on now?
    Answer: 1st floor (Calculation: 10 + (−6) + (−8) + 3 = 1)
  2. Solve: A store lost ₱1,500 on Monday (−1500), gained ₱4,000 on Tuesday (+4000), and lost ₱800 on Wednesday (−800). What was the net overall change?
    Answer: +₱1,700 profit (Calculation: −1500 + 4000 + (−800) = +1700)
