ILAW Lesson Plan: Mathematics

Topic 4.1: Discussion & Guided Activity Framework

Lesson Title 4.1 Classifying Terms Signaling Operations Grade / Quarter Grade 7 / Grade 11 — Term 1
Subject General Mathematics / Algebra Duration 1 Session (60 Minutes)

I. INTENTIONS

  • Learning Competency: Translates English phrases to mathematical expressions and vice versa.
  • Learning Objectives:
    1. Categorize key verbal phrases into basic mathematical operations (+, -, ×, ÷).
    2. Differentiate direct order phrases from "turn-around" phrases that alter variable placement.
    3. Collaboratively convert verbal problems into algebraic expressions through guided group discussions.

L. LEARNING CONTEXT

  • Target Misconception: Assuming that word order in English always reflects the left-to-right order of terms in algebraic expressions (e.g., writing 5 - x for "5 less than x").
  • Instructional Strategy: Guided inquiry and error-analysis discussion before individual execution.

A. ACTIVITY FLOW (60 Minutes)

1. Warm-Up & Hook: Rapid Term Sorting 10 Mins

Teacher Action: Displays 8 flashcards on the board (e.g., "increased by", "ratio of", "diminished by", "product of").

Student Action: Working in pairs, students quickly classify each card into four operation columns on their mini-whiteboards.

2. Interactive Discussion: Decoding Mathematical Keywords 20 Mins

Teacher facilitates an interactive lecture using the reference matrix below:

Operation Keywords / Signal Phrases Verbal Phrase Algebraic Translation
Addition (+) sum, increased by, more than, added to "a number x increased by 9" x + 9
Subtraction (-) difference, diminished by, less than, subtracted from "y diminished by 4" y - 4
Multiplication (×) product, times, multiplied by, twice, double "thrice a number n" 3n
Division (÷) quotient, divided by, ratio of, split into "ratio of k and 5" k / 5
💬 Discussion Prompt (Guided Inquiry):

Teacher: "Compare the two phrases: '10 less x' and '10 less than x'. Do they produce the same mathematical expression?"

Student Response & Analysis: No. '10 less x' means start with 10 and subtract x (10 - x). '10 less than x' means start with x and decrease it by 10 (x - 10).

⚠️ "Turn-Around" Rules Highlight: Phrases like "less than", "subtracted from", and "more than" flip the order of the terms.

3. Collaborative Discussion Activity: "Fix the Translation!" 18 Mins

Divide the class into small groups of 3 or 4. Hand each group a card containing common translation errors. Groups must identify the mistake, explain why it occurred, and write the correct expression.

  1. Case 1: "7 subtracted from w" was translated as 7 - w.
    Group Task: Correct the expression and explain the phrase order rule.
  2. Case 2: "The quotient of 8 and a number p" was translated as p / 8.
    Group Task: Correct the expression and discuss the role of word placement in division phrases.
  3. Case 3: "5 times the sum of x and 2" was translated as 5x + 2.
    Group Task: Identify where parentheses are required and present the revised answer.

4. Wrap-Up & Exit Slip 12 Mins

Students individually complete a 3-question exit ticket to verify mastery before moving to multi-step equations in the next session.
