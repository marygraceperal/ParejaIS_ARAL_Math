ILAW Lesson Plan: Mathematics

Topic 4.1: Mathematical Keywords & Phrases

Lesson Title 4.1 Classifying Terms Signaling Operations Grade / Quarter Grade 7 / Grade 11 — Term 1
Subject General Mathematics / Algebra Duration 1 Session (60 Minutes)

I. INTENTIONS

  • Learning Competency: Translates English phrases to mathematical expressions and vice versa.
  • Learning Objectives:
    1. Identify and classify key verbal phrases into their corresponding basic mathematical operations (+, -, ×, ÷).
    2. Recognize "turn-around" phrases (e.g., less than, subtracted from) that reverse the order of terms.
    3. Translate short English phrases into algebraic expressions with 100% accuracy in structured practice.

L. LEARNING CONTEXT

  • Learner Prior Knowledge: Students are familiar with basic arithmetic operations but often struggle with word problems due to vocabulary barriers and phrase order reversal.
  • Key Misconceptions to Address:
    • Confusing "5 less than x" (x - 5) with "5 less x" (5 - x).
    • Assuming the word order in English always matches the order of terms in the mathematical expression.
  • Learning Resources:
    • Operation Signal Word Classification Matrix
    • Student Activity Worksheet / Whiteboard Cards

A. ACTIVITY FLOW (60 Minutes)

1. Hook & Warm-Up: Word-to-Symbol Sorting (10 Minutes)

Distribute rapid-sort flashcards containing phrases like "increased by", "diminished by", "product of", and "quotient of". Students categorize them into four columns: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, and Division.

2. Direct Instruction: Operation Signal Words (15 Minutes)

Discuss the key signal phrases using the summary reference guide below:

Operation Signal Keywords & Phrases Algebraic Example
Addition (+) sum of, increased by, more than, added to, total, plus "5 more than x" → x + 5
Subtraction (-) difference of, decreased by, diminished by, less than, subtracted from, minus "y diminished by 8" → y - 8
Multiplication (×) product of, times, multiplied by, twice, double, thrice, fraction of "twice a number n" → 2n
Division (÷) quotient of, divided by, ratio of, split equally into "quotient of k and 4" → k / 4
⚠️ Critical Concept ("Turn-Around" Phrases): Phrases such as "less than" and "subtracted from" require changing the order of the terms.
Example: "10 subtracted from x" translates to x - 10 (NOT 10 - x).

3. Guided & Independent Practice (20 Minutes)

Students work in pairs to convert verbal phrases into algebraic expressions:

  • The quotient of a number $x$ and $7$ → x / 7
  • $12$ diminished by $m$ → 12 - m
  • $6$ less than three times $y$ → 3y - 6

4. Synthesis & Check for Understanding (15 Minutes)

Students complete a 5-item exit slip classifying operation keywords and writing expressions for two short word problems.

W. WAYS FORWARD

  • For Instruction: Review exit slip results. If over 30% of students make errors on "turn-around" phrases, start the next session with a 5-minute dedicated drill on "less than" vs "less".
  • Intervention: Provide remedial flashcards for learners struggling with vocabulary recognition.
  • Next Topic Link: Transition to Topic 4.2: Translating Two-Step Algebraic Equations and Inequalities.
