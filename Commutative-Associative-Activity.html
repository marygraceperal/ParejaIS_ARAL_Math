Student Activity: Commutative & Associative Properties

Topic 2.1: Mental Math via Rearranging and Regrouping Terms

📌 Instructions

Complete the following exercises using mental math techniques. Show your rearranged or regrouped expressions on the lines provided to demonstrate how you arrived at the answer efficiently.

Part I: Identify the Property

1. State whether each equation demonstrates the Commutative Property or the Associative Property:

a) (18 + 42) + 15 = 18 + (42 + 15)

b) 25 × 14 = 14 × 25

c) 5 × (20 × 9) = (5 × 20) × 9

Part II: Mental Addition Shortcuts

2. Rearrange and regroup the terms to evaluate mentally:

a) 34 + 57 + 66 + 43

b) 125 + 89 + 75 + 111

Part III: Mental Multiplication Shortcuts

3. Rearrange and regroup the factors to evaluate mentally:

a) 4 × 17 × 25

b) 8 × 13 × 125

Part IV: Real-World Application

4. A store owner bought items priced at ₱135, ₱62, ₱65, and ₱138. Show how to calculate the total cost mentally using compatible pairs.
